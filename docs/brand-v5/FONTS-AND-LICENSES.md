# Fonts and licenses — v5 candidate

**Selected:** Inter Display Bold for GOEGSA; Inter Display Medium for English tagline. Both fonts are converted to vector paths in delivered SVGs. They are not bundled or loaded by end-user devices. The wordmark has tightened tracking and small kerning adjustments, but **these are NOT custom-designed glyphs**.

## Three evaluated open-license choices

1. **Inter Display Bold** — selected. Source file: `/usr/share/fonts/opentype/inter/InterDisplay-Bold.otf`. Version is recorded in `reports/font-versions.json`. License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/ . Local license text: `/usr/share/doc/fonts-inter/copyright`.
2. **Lato Heavy** — alternative with a softer humanist shape. Source: `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/lato/Lato-Heavy.ttf`. Version in report. License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/ . Local license text: `/usr/share/doc/fonts-lato/copyright`.
3. **Noto Sans Bold** — alternative, robust but visually more neutral. Source: `/usr/share/fonts/truetype/noto/NotoSans-Bold.ttf`. Version in report. License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/ . Local copyright metadata: `/usr/share/doc/fonts-noto-core/copyright` (if installed).

These licenses allow use and modification subject to their terms. The complete locally installed license/copyright notices are copied into `reference/font-license-notices/` where available. This file documents provenance, not an assertion that a custom proprietary typeface was created.
