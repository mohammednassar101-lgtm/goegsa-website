# GOEGSA Vector Candidate v5

**STATUS: CANDIDATE — NOT APPROVED.** No files named final or approved.

Official name: GOEGSA. English tagline: SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT & DIGITAL SOLUTIONS. Suggested Arabic copy only: تطوير البرمجيات والحلول الرقمية (NOT rendered in the logo).

## What changed
1. All PNG, ICO and BMP assets are generated from the supplied v5 SVGs. Source and tool for each output are listed in `reports/file-manifest.json`. No unrelated image is substituted for icon-32.
2. Inter Display Bold replaces DejaVu Sans Bold. The letter outlines are font-derived with tracked/adjusted spacing, **not bespoke letter designs**. See FONTS-AND-LICENSES.md.
3. Dedicated simplified G micro mark for 16/24/32 pixels, with no pixels or arrow. Full mark at 48+; favicon always micro.
4. Single palette in brand-tokens.json. Light background #F4F7F5 and dark #121A15 are carried forward from the earlier identity decision. Gradient stops are listed there; the two modes use contrast-adjusted solid accent details.
5. Each horizontal lockup exists with and without tagline. Use tagline only when logo height >=64px. At 18/24/32px use wordmark without tagline or standalone micro mark.

## Placement
Company developer credits only (splash, dashboard developer mark, About, installer, company site and social). Never apply the company colors to school product UI or customer documents. No trademark registration claim.

## Safe use
Clear space around lockups: at least 1/5 symbol height. Do not stretch, skew, alter colors or add glow. SVG paths have no live text or raster images. All output remains candidate pending human visual, trademark, and integration review.

## Limits
No exact reproduction of glossy concept image; this is a vector interpretation. Browser/Inkscape cross-rendering and trademark similarity have not been independently confirmed. Human Arabic logo typesetting remains outstanding.
