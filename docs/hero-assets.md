# Homepage hero imagery

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Images are illustrative lifestyle scenes, not actual customers or product screenshots. Optimized WebP copies are included in the Site source.

## Assets
- `dist/assets/hero-school.webp`: school administrator and teacher collaborating at a laptop in a bright contemporary school office.
- `dist/assets/hero-commerce.webp`: small business owner and colleague preparing a customer order with a laptop in a home-goods store.
- `dist/assets/hero-mobile.webp`: two professionals reviewing a smartphone in a bright shared workspace.

## Final prompt set
Shared brief: ads-marketing; premium natural daylight editorial photography for GOEGSA, a software company serving Arabic-speaking markets. Soft white, emerald and teal accents, neutral wood, green plants, realistic skin and hands. Wide landscape framing with centrally located subjects and room for responsive crops. No text, logos, watermarks or collages. Device screens are oblique and non-legible, not a product interface.

1. Welcoming contemporary school office in the Arab region. An adult female school administrator in professional modest clothing and an adult male teacher collaborate at a laptop. Calm organized paperwork and warm natural expressions. Symbolizes easier school administration, without depicting a real client.
2. Contemporary small local craft and home-goods store. An Arab business owner works with a laptop while a colleague arranges a neatly packaged customer order. Authentic interaction and organized shelves. Symbolizes websites and stores helping business operations, without depicting a verified result.
3. Bright comfortable coworking studio. Two adult Arabic-speaking professionals in modest business attire review a smartphone together with a laptop nearby. Focused collaboration and everyday convenience. Symbolizes mobile applications and business software, without representing an actual product screenshot.

## Motion
Horizontal slide transitions and subtle CSS pan/zoom. No video generation. Pause/play, hover/focus suspension, visibility suspension and reduced-motion support are implemented.
